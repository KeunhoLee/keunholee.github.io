Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Overview

Obsidian Rclone is a personal application for synchronizing Obsidian notes to Google Drive.

Data Access and Usage

The application uses Google Drive API authorization to upload, read, and update files managed by Rclone.

Data Storage

Notes are stored locally and copied to the user's own Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored on the user's device.

Data Sharing

This application does not independently transmit user data to third-party servers. Files may be accessed by other services separately authorized by the user.

Data Control

Users can delete synchronized files from Google Drive and revoke application access through their Google Account settings.
